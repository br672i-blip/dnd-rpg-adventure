An adventure without an edge

The map keeps generating as you walk, and monsters get tougher the farther you travel from home.

Towns, quests and loot

Every realm has a town to rest, shop and take on work from the locals.

  • Buy and sell gear at the shop, and upgrade it at the blacksmith's forge
  • Take quests from villagers and track them in your journal
  • Trade Soul Shards for blessings at the temple

Turn-based battles

Walk into a monster and the fight begins. Pick your moves carefully, because the enemy hits back.

  • Attack, cast Fireball, heal, use items or run
  • Goblins and wolves near home, dragons and vampires far away
  • Level up for more health and mana, and unlock a class ultimate at level 10

Real-time dungeons

Dungeons switch to real-time action: move, strike and dodge through two rooms of enemies, then claim the chests.

  • Abandoned Kingdom, Cursed Crypt, Bandit Hideout and Dragon's Lair
  • Pillars and cover to fight around
  • Better rewards in higher-tier dungeons

Four realms, one final boss

Each portal leads somewhere more dangerous. At the end of the road waits Solgareth, the Fallen, in a sealed arena with no escape.

Realm I

Overworld

Meadows, forests and deserts full of goblins, wolves, orcs and trolls.

Realm II

Nether

Ash and lava, ruled by imps, demons and fire giants.

Realm III

Void

A starless dark where wraiths, shadow beasts and void lords hunt.

Realm IV

Celestial

Fallen angels and seraphs guard the way to the final battle.

Choose your hero

Then pick a difficulty from Easy to Nightmare.

Warrior

The most health and strength. Raises a Shield Block to soak hits, and unleashes Whirlwind at level 10.

Mage

Fragile but deadly, with by far the most mana for fireballs and healing. Calls down a Meteor at level 10.

Rogue

Fast, with the most stamina and dexterity. Escapes with Super Dash and strikes from the dark with Shadow Strike at level 10.

Good to know

The first load takes a few seconds while the game engine downloads. After that it starts quickly.

Saves are stored in your browser, with three slots. Clearing your browser data erases them, and they don't carry over between browsers or devices.

Prefer a desktop window? Run it with Python:

# needs Python 3.12 or newer
git clone https://github.com/br672i-blip/dnd-rpg-adventure
cd dnd-rpg-adventure
pip install -r requirements.txt
python pygame_DnD_RPG_infinite_map_v2.py
